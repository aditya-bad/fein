## What happened and why
- Revenue grew from ₹248 cr (FY19) to ₹516 cr (FY24) and ₹735 cr (FY25). EBITDA rose from ₹23 cr to ₹99 cr (FY24) and ₹127 cr (FY25). PAT rose from ₹8 cr to ₹67 cr (FY24), about 8x. Margins recovered to 16–19% and ROCE to 23–25%.

- It migrated to the main board. Liquidity and institutional discovery improved and the P/E rerated from ~19x to 30–40x.
- Watch-outs later: FY26 revenue was flat, there was US tariff impact on exports, and receivable and inventory days stayed very high (CCC ~250 days).
