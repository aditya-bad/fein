## Key events
- Sep 2017: the stock hit lower circuits on speculation about debt stress and sales of pledged shares. Promoter pledge rose from ~44% to ~58% of the promoter's holding by Sep 2017 (Trendlyne).
- FY19: revenue collapsed from ₹7,320 cr (FY18) to ₹1,370 cr. Net loss was −₹3,216 cr, and receivables and other assets were written off (other assets fell from ~₹2,960 cr to ~₹135 cr). The company said it faced "an issue of receivables management".
- 2019–2020: lenders declared defaults. KKR India Financial Services took it to NCLT over a >₹1,200 cr default. Total debt was ~₹2,000 cr, mostly NPA. CBI registered a bank-fraud case (~₹1,400 cr, SBI-led consortium). Corporate insolvency followed (IBBI claims filings 2021).
- SEBI's order covering Dec 2016–Dec 2018 found fictitious revenue and misreported receivables to lenders and auditors. It fined former executives ~₹375 cr in total (LiveLaw PDF).

## Cause / what was visible at T
Eight straight years of negative free cash flow despite rising reported profits. ~90-day receivables in a business whose suppliers are paid in days. Borrowings kept rising with "growth". A pledged promoter. Revenue was dominated by bulk B2B and trading-like sales. Fixed assets were tiny relative to revenue until FY17. A sudden large capitalisation in FY17. Reported profit was never backed by cash.
