## What happened and why
- Mid-2019: bought Plateau Excavation (Atlanta site development) for ~$400m, which moved the mix decisively to "E-Infrastructure" (large site development for data centres, warehouses and manufacturing). 2021: bought Petillo (New Jersey site work).
- Revenue grew from $1.04bn (2018) to $2.12bn (2024). Operating income rose from $43m to $265m. EPS went from $0.93 to $8.27 (2024 included a one-off gain on the RHB sale). Gross margin went from ~10.6% to ~20%+.
- Data-centre and onshoring capex (2022–2024) became a huge tailwind for site-development work. The low-bid highway business shrank in relevance.
- Valuation: rerated from ~5–6x EBITDA / ~13x earnings to 20x+ earnings as the market reclassified it from a "road contractor" to a data-centre-exposed infrastructure name.
- In 2019 it released the deferred-tax valuation allowance (~$26m tax benefit), visible as negative tax expense.
- Lesson: an outside CEO's margin-first mix shift, a clean balance sheet, NOL-sheltered cash and a cheap multiple made a strong base. The 5x+ came from the strategy meeting a secular capex wave.
