## What happened and why
- FY17: comps fell 11% and net sales/sq ft dropped from $545 to $482. Operating income fell from $68m to $30m. Q2 FY17 (Jun 2017) was a big miss and the stock halved.
- FY18: operating loss of −$34m. FY19: operating loss of −$24m, plus a reverse split. The company kept opening boutiques in FY17 (to 721) right into a mall-traffic collapse, then had to close them.
- CEO turnover continued: Lawrence left in early 2019, and new leadership couldn't fix merchandising. COVID (Mar 2020) closed stores. Chapter 11 followed in Dec 2020 and liquidation/sale in Jan 2021.
- Cause: a fashion-dependent, mall-dependent concept with falling sales productivity since FY12. Margin compression had already started in FY14, which buybacks masked at the EPS level. Large off-balance-sheet lease commitments turned "net cash, no debt" into high operating leverage. Leadership churn came at the worst time.
- Lesson: low P/E and EV/EBIT plus net cash isn't safety when sales per square foot are in structural decline and leases are the real debt.
