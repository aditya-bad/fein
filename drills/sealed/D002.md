## Key events
- Apr–May 2017: the company delayed its 10-Q and announced that it would restate FY2016 and FY2017 results. Its auditor, BKD, withdrew its reports back to the period ended June 30, 2016. The cause was the valuation of used tractors traded with 19th Capital, the leasing JV formed with Element Fleet. NYSE delisted the stock and it moved to OTC.
- 2017–2019: Celadon never filed restated financials. Lenders kept it alive through repeated forbearance. The SEC and DOJ investigated.
- Dec 2019: Celadon filed Chapter 11 and shut down, one of the largest truckload-carrier failures in US history (~3,800 drivers). The equity was worthless.
- 2019–2020: the SEC charged that Celadon traded trucks at inflated prices to avoid recording impairments (~$20m+ of losses hidden). Two former executives were charged criminally. The company admitted the accounting fraud and agreed to $42.2m in restitution (deferred prosecution).

Q1–Q2 FY17 operating losses ran alongside management's optimism.
