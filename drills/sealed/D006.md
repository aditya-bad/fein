## What happened and why
- COVID shut schools. FY21 revenue fell 44% to ₹832 cr and FY22 margins dropped to 8%. Recovery came in FY23 (₹1,694 cr, 17% margin), after which revenue was flat at ~₹1,750–1,790 cr through FY26.
- Stationery exports grew, but publishing stayed ex-growth. ROCE fell from 25% (FY19) to 15–19%.
- FY25 had a large one-off other income (₹707 cr; EPS ₹34), mainly from selling a minority investment in an unlisted K-12 school company. That gave a pop but no structural rerating.
- Lesson: a high-ROCE niche franchise with no growth runway, at a fair (~16x) P/E, gives roughly market-like returns. There's no fraud and no multibagger.
