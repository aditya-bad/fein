## What happened and why
- Revenue grew from ₹1,240 cr (FY19) to ₹3,160 cr (FY24) and ₹4,265 cr (FY26). EBITDA rose from ₹62 cr to ₹287 cr (FY24) and ₹439 cr (FY26). PAT rose from ₹19 cr to ₹242 cr (FY24), about 12x.
- ROCE went from 13% to 28–32% (FY22–FY24). Margins stabilised at 8–10% as the value-added mix and overseas share rose. Tax rate fell to ~10–15%.
- India formalised recycling: Battery Waste Management Rules 2022 introduced EPR obligations and favoured formal recyclers. The company also diversified into aluminium, plastics and rubber.
- Valuation: P/E rerated from ~10–25x on trough earnings to 40–60x on much larger earnings. Promoters sold some stake, and in FY25 a ~₹1,000 cr QIP raised equity.
- Lesson: the FY19 earnings dip and the 68% price drawdown from the 2018 peak hid a scalable, regulation-tailwind franchise. Starting valuation on normalised earnings was low.
