# D015 — Blind analyst answer (set 2)

## 1. Recognition declaration
**Recognised as a US family-controlled, dual-class electronic components maker that bought a power-solutions business and a connectivity division with debt (I believe Bel Fuse), high confidence. Exclude.** I also have a rough memory that the stock re-rated strongly in the following years, which contaminates any probability I give.

## 2. Business understanding
- It makes broad-line passive, power and interconnect components with contract-like OEM and distributor relationships. Gross margins are modest, and operating margins of 3–5% are weak for the sector, a sign of a sub-scale and inefficient power business.
- Cash conversion is good: CFO is positive every year, FCF/NI is often above 1, and the losses were non-cash impairments. Cash goes to a ~2% dividend and to paying down debt.

## 3. Bull / bear / variant
- **Bull:** P/B of 0.9 and EV/EBITDA of 7. Y0 FCF was 83 against a market cap of 335 (a 25% yield; ~45 average, ~13%). The restructuring and footprint moves are done. Record backlog, a component shortage giving pricing power, and new EV, renewables and datacom demand. If operating margin goes from 4% to 9–10% on ~1,000–1,100 of revenue, EBIT is ~100 and NI ~75, against 26 today. At 13–15×, market cap would be ~1,000+, about 3×. The mechanism (industry shortage, lead times) can be verified from outside through distributor and peer data.
- **Bear:** a family voting lock means no takeover discipline. There have been two impairment rounds, revenue is below Y-5 and the cyclical shortage may produce a double-ordering bust. Margins could stay at 4–5%.
- **Variant view:** the market is anchored on a decade of disappointment and has not priced in operating leverage on a cleaned-up cost base at the start of an up-cycle.
- **Benign flags:** the impairments are non-cash and CFO stayed positive. The dual class has not been abused (steady dividends, no dilution).
- **Real flags:** structurally low margins and backlog inflated by double-ordering.
- **Green flags I distrust:** a record backlog in a shortage, which is partly phantom.

## 4. Valuation
Normalised FCF is ~45–60, so FCF yield is ~13–18%. The market is pricing in no improvement. Fair value is ~1.3–1.8× the current price even without margin expansion.

## 5. Probabilities
- **O1 ≥3×: 0.25.** Cheap on cash flow, with an up-cycle and operating leverage; it needs margins to double.
- **O2 ≤0.5×: 0.10.** Already at P/B 0.9 with strong cash flow.
- **O3: 0.02.** Leverage is moderate and cash generation steady.

## 6. Decision
**OWN (small), conviction 3.**
