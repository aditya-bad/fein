# D016 — Blind analyst answer (set 2)

## 1. Recognition declaration
**Suspected recognition (~50%) as an Indian ferrous foundry supplying commercial-vehicle and tractor makers with a ~74.8% promoter stake and a new export-oriented plant (possibly Nelcast). Exclude.** I do not recall its later price path with any confidence, so contamination is limited, but the suspicion is enough to exclude it.

## 2. Business understanding
- It sells castings by weight. Margins depend on scrap and pig-iron pass-through, which lags, and on utilisation. It is cyclical with domestic commercial vehicles and agriculture.
- The new plant (~545 of capex) doubled the debt. ROCE fell to 5% because the plant is not yet utilised and the domestic cycle is at a trough.

## 3. Bull / bear / variant
- **Bull:** the trough of the domestic CV cycle, strong tractor demand, overseas truck makers diversifying their supply base, and a new plant that is already paid for. If revenue reaches ~2,500 at 11% EBITDA, PAT is ~150. Y0 CFO of 191 shows cash arriving.
- **Bear:** peers are adding the same export capacity, and foundry export margins are thin. Receivable days jumped to 97 (on export terms or delayed collections). EV/EBITDA is 19× on trough earnings. The historic ROCE ceiling is ~14%, and the promoter float is tiny.
- **Variant view:** this is a mediocre-ROCE business in which the expansion lifts the size but not the quality.
- **Benign flags:** the trough P/E of 72× is a cyclical artefact (L10), and the high debt follows a completed project with free cash flow turning positive (143).
- **Real flags:** the receivable days, and a structural ROCE barely above the cost of capital.
- **Green flags I distrust:** management's "return to earlier margins" target, which were only 8–11%.

## 4. Valuation
On normalised PAT of ~85 (Y-2), the P/E is ~17×. A 3× move needs a market cap of ~4,400, or PAT of ~250 at 18×. That means revenue nearly doubling at peak margins — possible, but it is not the base case.

## 5. Probabilities
- **O1 ≥3×: 0.12.** Base-rate-like: cycle plus new plant, offset by low quality.
- **O2 ≤0.5×: 0.13.** Trough-cycle entry and a strong promoter cushion the downside.
- **O3: 0.02.** Clean governance and serviceable debt.

## 6. Decision
**WATCH, conviction 2.**
