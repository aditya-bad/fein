# D020 — Blind analyst answer (set 2)

## 1. Recognition declaration
**Recognised as a US mattress-fabric and upholstery-fabric maker that bought a window-treatment maker and an online bedding retailer (I believe Culp Inc.), high confidence. Exclude.** I have a rough memory that it declined afterwards.

## 2. Business understanding
- It is a B2B fabric supplier to a few large furniture and bedding makers, with low pricing power against imports. Profit depends on volume and on raw-material and freight costs.
- Revenue has been flat for seven years, and EBITDA margin has drifted from 12% to 6%. Cash conversion is decent: FCF is positive in every year. Cash goes to dividends and small buybacks, and it holds net cash.

## 3. Bull / bear / variant
- **Bull:** net cash of ~46 is ~25% of the market cap, and P/B is 1.3. Taxed normally, the P/E is ~21×. A flexible Asian and near-shore platform, plus the online business.
- **Bear:** Y0 is a peak-demand year for home furnishings with the pandemic still running. Management itself guides softer and expects price increases to lag. There is a structural decline in margins and ROCE (from 20% to 8%). If demand rotates back to services, EBITDA could go back to the Y-1 level near zero. The online acquisitions have not been proven.
- **Variant view:** none. Strong cash flow in a decline phase does not prevent the stock from falling.
- **Benign flags:** the 70% tax rate (a structural mismatch of losses and profits across countries), the Y-1 impairments and the credit-line draw (repaid).
- **Real flags:** the long secular margin decline and peak-demand earnings.
- **Green flags I distrust:** the Y0 revenue growth of +17%.

## 4. Valuation
EV of ~150 is ~7× peak-ish EBITDA of ~21. On mid-cycle EBITDA of ~15–20 it is not cheap for a business that is shrinking. Fair value is ~70–100% of the current price.

## 5. Probabilities
- **O1 ≥3×: 0.03.**
- **O2 ≤0.5×: 0.38.** Demand normalises and margins compress further; the cash only buys time.
- **O3: 0.04.** Net cash makes insolvency unlikely within 5 years unless losses are prolonged.

## 6. Decision
**AVOID, conviction 3.**
