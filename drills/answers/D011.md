# D011 — Blind analyst answer (set 2)

## 1. Recognition declaration
**Suspected recognition — probably a listed Indian maker of electrical steel laminations/stampings and machined parts for motor and generator makers (my guess is Pitti Engineering, ~60% confidence). Exclude from scoring.** The features that point there: stampings for power-generation, motor and transport OEMs, a third to a half of revenue exported, the stock up 5× in a year at a pandemic-era T, about 11.6k shareholders. I do not remember its numbers well, but I have a rough idea that the stock did well later, so the probabilities below are reasoned from the packet and may still be tainted.

## 2. Business understanding
- It is a contract manufacturer whose revenue depends on volume. Customers need years to qualify a supplier and stick with one afterwards, so switching costs are moderate. It has little pricing power on steel, which is passed through with a lag.
- Profit depends on how much of the Y-3 capacity it uses. That expansion doubled fixed assets, and the plant is reported to be well below full. Depreciation (~15) and interest (~15) together are about 80% of EBITDA (~39), so the business is highly leveraged both operationally and financially.
- Cash goes into working capital: inventory of 130–260 days plus debtors of about 120 days works out to roughly 6 months of sales. Free cash flow summed over seven years is about zero. ROCE of 12–17% is barely above the cost of debt.

## 3. Bull / bear / variant
- **Bull:** spare capacity is already paid for. If revenue doubles to ~500 with little capex and EBITDA reaches 16–17%, EBITDA is ~80–85 and PAT is ~38–40, against ~14 today (part of which is one-off). Export customers are moving supply away from one dominant low-cost country, a mechanism outsiders can check through customer announcements and export data. The domestic capex cycle is reviving, and the move into machined assemblies raises value per kg.
- **Bear:** Y0 PAT includes ~9.5 of one-off other income. Without it, PAT is ~7–8 and the real P/E is ~30×. Debt rose to 158 in a lockdown year, inventory went back up to 171 days, and free cash flow was −15. A 1.3× debt/equity ratio and rising steel prices make a margin squeeze plus a working-capital blow-out a real risk. The stock has already re-rated 5×.
- **Variant view:** the market may be extrapolating from a starting point that is better than it looks. Revenue is still below Y-2 (311), so the growth is recovery into capacity that already exists, not new spend. That is the main asymmetry.
- **Flags I judge benign (L10/L12):** high debt/equity and negative free cash flow happened in an expansion phase; the debt is visible bank debt and the capex is real (fixed assets doubled). The inventory days look like the norm for this business model. The promoter stake fell by 10 points and was then rebuilt. The mechanism was not verified, but the stake was rebuilt rather than sold down.
- **Real flags:** aggregate free cash flow is zero, and Y0 earnings quality is weak because of the one-offs. Institutions have left, so no outsider is checking the numbers.
- **Green flags I distrust:** the P/E of 17× (inflated by one-offs) and the "doubling revenue without capex" plan (management's word only).

## 4. Valuation
EV of ~403 is ~10× EBITDA and ~2.1× book. On normalised PAT of ~8, the P/E is ~30×, which prices in a meaningful part of the recovery. If the bull case arrives (PAT ~35–40 by Y4 at 15–18×), market cap is 550–700, about 2.3–2.9×, with upside if it re-rates further. Fair value today is roughly the current price. The upside depends on getting volume back into the existing capacity.

## 5. Probabilities (5y after Y0)
- **O1 ≥3×: 0.22.** The operating leverage is real and a thinly followed stock can re-rate, but the 5× rally has already absorbed part of it.
- **O2 ≤0.5×: 0.20.** About the base rate. Leverage, steel inflation and the post-rally starting point offset the low capex need.
- **O3: 0.04.** No governance or audit flags, and the debt is serviceable at the current EBITDA.

## 6. Decision
**WATCH, conviction 2.** I would want to see capacity utilisation and export orders confirmed in 2–3 quarters of data before owning it after a 5× run.
