# D006 — Blind analyst answer (T = mid-July 2019)

## 1. Recognition
**Recognised as Navneet Education (exclude).** A state-board guide publisher in two western states with export stationery is identifiable. I have partial memory of its later trajectory, so the drill is contaminated. The analysis below is from the packet only.

## 2. Business
- **Publishing:** content is created once and reprinted yearly. Gross margins are high (EBITDA likely 30%+). The constraints are print-run forecasting and returns.
  - Distribution goes through thousands of book retailers on credit, which drives receivables and returns.
  - Syllabus changes force new editions. That is good for sales but costly in write-offs.
- **Stationery:** a paper-converting business with commodity paper input and thin margins (~8–12%). The export side depends on a few big US retailers placing annual orders.
- Where cash goes: inventory of ~9 months (paper bought ahead, books printed ahead), dividends and buybacks, and the digital arm's losses.

## 3. Cases and flags
- **Bull:** a 25–35% ROCE franchise at a P/E of 16x. The syllabus-revision cycle is visible, the brand is sticky, and the promoters return cash. If export stationery and digital work, low-teens EPS growth plus a re-rating to ~22x could double the stock.
- **Bear:**
  1. State boards are losing urban share to national boards, while free and low-cost digital content and possible regulation threaten guide books.
  2. EBITDA has gone nowhere: ~₹240 cr in FY15 vs ~₹270 cr in FY19, about 3% CAGR.
  3. The margin is diluting from 25% to 19% as low-margin stationery grows.
  4. Debt more than doubled while dividend payout was cut from 40% to 15%.
  5. Export customer concentration.
- **Variant perception:** the syllabus-driven FY19 growth is a one-off replacement bump being taken as a trend. Once it passes, revenue could fall.
- **Flags I think are benign:**
  1. Inventory of ~280 days, which is structural to seasonal publishing.
  2. Lumpy debtor days, from season timing.
- **Red flags I keep:** CFO conversion has weakened. CFO/PAT was ~1.3x in FY15–16 and ~0.5x in FY17–19. Rising working-capital debt for a commodity export business erodes the quality of the franchise.
- **Green flags I distrust:** ROCE, because the publishing ROCE is likely shrinking within the blend, and the buybacks, now done partly with debt.

## 4. Valuation
- At EV ₹2,700 cr (10x EBITDA), the price implies ~4–6% growth, a fair base case for a slowly eroding franchise.
- Fair value: ₹85–140.
- A 3x needs ~₹7,300 cr of market cap, around ₹16 EPS at 20x. That's very unlikely given ~3% EBITDA growth.

## 5. Probabilities
- **O1 = 0.06.** A mature, structurally challenged core with no visible growth engine.
- **O2 = 0.18.** Roughly the base rate. Regulatory or board-shift risk and margin dilution could halve it, but the balance sheet is fine.
- **O3 = 0.02.** Low leverage, a long record of dividends and buybacks and no governance flags.

## 6. Decision
**WATCH** (or avoid as a multibagger candidate). Conviction 2.
