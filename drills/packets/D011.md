## 3. Business and context known before T
- Customers are a concentrated set of large domestic and multinational equipment makers (power generation, industrial motors, transport). Long qualification cycles; sticky once approved.
- Management's stated plan (Y0 commentary; builder's paraphrase, not verified line by line): move up the value chain from basic stampings into machined and fully assembled components, grow export share, lift capacity utilisation of the Y-3 expansion (reported as well below full), and use the extra capacity to roughly double revenue over a few years without proportionate capex.
- Sector backdrop at T: domestic capex cycle widely described as weak for a decade but showing early signs of revival; global customers talking about diversifying supply away from a single dominant low-cost supplier country; input steel prices rising sharply.
- Working capital is heavy (inventory 130–260 days historically; debtors 100–130 days) and debt/equity has stayed above 1x throughout; FCF has been lumpy and roughly zero in aggregate over the seven years.
- No auditor qualification known to the builder. No known regulatory action.
