# Drill D006 — point-in-time packet

**Decision date T:** 2019, Q3 (mid-July 2019)
**Market:** India (NSE/BSE)
**Sector (generic):** Mid-cap education publisher and stationery maker. It publishes supplementary school-learning books (guides, workbooks, practice material) for state-board students in two large western Indian states. It also makes paper stationery (notebooks etc.) for the domestic market and for export to large overseas retailers, and runs a small loss-making digital-education arm.
**Currency:** ₹ crore unless stated. Consolidated, rounded. Fiscal year ends March.

## 3. Business and context known before T
- Publishing moat: decades-old brand among state-board students and teachers, a large distribution network of book retailers, and frequent syllabus revisions that force new editions. One of the two home states revised its school syllabus in phases over 2017–2019, which drove a replacement cycle and helped FY19 publishing growth.
- Headwinds discussed at the time: possible state-level restrictions on guide books, free/low-cost digital content, CBSE/ICSE (national boards) gaining share from state boards in cities, and concentration of export stationery among a few large US retail customers. Notebooks were moved into the GST system (2017).
- Management statements: digital arm losses to narrow, growth in export stationery with new customers, capital allocation through dividends and buybacks.
