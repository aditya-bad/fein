## 3. Business and context known before T
- One of the two or three largest readership newspaper groups in its language belt. Most revenue is advertising, with circulation second. A national-election year (2019) normally lifts political and government advertising.
- Majority-owned FM-radio subsidiary, separately listed. Expanded radio licences in recent government auctions.
- Industry: Indian print ad growth has slowed to low single digits. Digital is taking share of advertising budgets. Newsprint (largely imported) rose sharply in FY19 with a weak rupee. Peers have cut pagination and cover-price discounts.
- Management commentary (FY19): newsprint cost pressure expected to ease, focus on cost control and digital monetisation. Buybacks are the stated way to return surplus cash.
- Balance sheet: modest borrowings against sizeable treasury investments. Low net debt.
- Auditor: a mid-to-large Indian audit firm. No qualification known to the packet builder.
- No public governance red flags known to the packet builder.
