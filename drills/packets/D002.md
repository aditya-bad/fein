## 4. Business and context known before T
- Growth strategy FY13–FY16: buy many small truckload carriers (often distressed). Expand the tractor fleet sharply with new tractors financed by capital leases. Run an in-house leasing and equipment-sales arm that leases tractors to owner-operators and sells used tractors.
- Industry: 2016 was a US freight recession. Truckload rates were soft, capacity was too high, and used-tractor prices fell sharply (industry reports of large double-digit declines in used Class 8 values during 2016). Peers reported falling earnings and gains on sale.
- Dec 30, 2016: the company closed a joint venture with a large fleet-management/leasing company. It contributed its leasing-tractor portfolio (>10,000 tractors across the combined JV) and holds ~50% equity. It received ~$58m net cash at closing, used to repay debt. Separately it sold ~$50m of equipment to the JV at net book value. The JV is not consolidated; an investment in JV of ~$100m is on the balance sheet.
- Management framing: the leasing JV makes the company more asset-light and pays down debt. Operating improvement is expected as the freight market normalises.
- Auditor: a regional (non-Big-4) accounting firm.
