## 3. Business and context known before T
- Management narrative: large growth runway from a multi-year mining-services/coal-evacuation contract with a state-owned miner (ramp-up drove FY18–FY19 revenue growth), plus expansion of rail and port assets. Revenue expected to keep growing strongly as the contract and terminals ramp up.
- Promoter-group context: the group's founder and chairman controls a listed consumer-retail company and other businesses (real estate, hospitality, financial investments). The group is highly leveraged at the holding level. A tax-authority investigation of the group was publicly reported in late 2017. In early 2019 the group agreed to sell a large financial investment, with group deleveraging among the stated aims. The parent has been funding this company with unsecured loans (see notes).
- Industry: Indian port and logistics volumes are growing with coal and container trade. Asset-heavy logistics returns are low, and government contracts carry payment delays.
