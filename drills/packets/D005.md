## 4. Business and context known before T
- CEO since 2015 (hired from outside the company) laid out a multi-year plan. Stop chasing low-margin hard-bid highway work, raise margins, and diversify into higher-margin "specialty" niches (residential foundations, design-build, water, aviation). Targets were framed mainly as gross-margin expansion.
- Strategy direction (as of early 2019): keep shifting the mix towards higher-margin work, consider further acquisitions in higher-margin niches, keep bid discipline.
- Industry: US federal highway funding was secured by the FAST Act (2015–2020). Several states raised fuel taxes for road funding in 2017–18. Sun Belt housing starts were recovering but growth slowed in late 2018 as mortgage rates rose. Heavy-civil contractors are generally low-multiple, low-margin businesses with fixed-price bid risk.
- Auditor: a national (non-Big-4) firm. Clean opinion, and ICFR deemed effective.
- Risks noted at the time: concentration in a few states, fixed-price contract risk (history of large losses in 2013), housing-cycle exposure in the new segment, acquisition integration.
