## 3. Business and context known before T
- Management commentary (Y0 annual report, paraphrased): record demand for home furnishings during the pandemic lifted sales in both fabric businesses; flexible Asian/near-shore manufacturing platform; strong balance sheet; expects the next quarter to be softer because of raw-material and freight inflation, labour shortages and supply-chain disruption, with price increases lagging; investing in the online consumer business.
- Long-term record: revenue flat for seven years (~310–355 units), margins drifting down from ~12% EBITDA (Y-4) to ~6% (Y0); ROCE fell from ~20% to single digits.
- Customer base concentrated in a few large furniture and bedding makers; low-cost imported fabric competition is a constant pressure.
- Sector backdrop at T: home-goods demand at a pandemic-driven peak; consumer spending expected to rotate back to services; shipping costs at record highs; rates near zero but inflation rising.
