## 3. Business and context known before T
- The print segment is a cash generator with low capex. The business-forms industry is in long-term decline (digital invoicing/payments), but niche products (checks, tags, labels, envelopes, short-run custom work) decline more slowly. Distributors value broad catalogues and fast turnaround.
- Capital allocation track record: steady dividend; serial small print acquisitions at low multiples; the apparel business was bought years earlier as a diversification move.
- Management commentary (FY2015 10-K): continue bolt-on print acquisitions, cut costs in apparel, focus on cash flow and dividends.
- Industry/macro (2015): strong dollar, low cotton prices, intense blank-apparel price competition. US small-business formation is steady.
- Auditor: a national (non-Big-4) firm. Clean opinion.
