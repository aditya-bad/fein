## 3. Business and context known before T
- Positioning (from company materials): one of few Indian makers of large engineered water-control gates and valves with international certifications. The acquired developed-market brand (100+ years old) brings specifications, references and access to municipal tenders in that market. The plan is to make much of that product in low-cost Indian plants and ship it.
- Order book: management cited a growing order book, with export orders a rising share (exact figure at T not compiled).
- Industry context: Indian government water programmes (urban water supply/sewerage missions, river clean-up, irrigation/canal modernisation) increasingly tender modern gates and screens. In developed markets, ageing water infrastructure needs gate/valve replacement. Competition comes from a few global specialists and small local fabricators.
- Risks noted at the time: micro-cap with thin liquidity, lumpy project revenue, long receivable cycle, overseas integration, government-customer payment delays.
