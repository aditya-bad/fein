## 4. Business and context known before T
- Management guidance for FY17: net sales of $527–543m, comparable sales flat to up low single digits, 60–65 new boutiques and 10–15 closures. Management believes the US can support 900+ boutiques.
- Leadership: the chairman/CEO resigned in May 2016. A new CEO (an experienced specialty-apparel executive) started Oct 2016, and the CFO role is filled on an interim basis. In 2012 the company dismissed a CFO over improper disclosure of company information on social media.
- Strategy statements: "invigorate merchandising" with more newness and an open-to-buy approach to chase trends, optimise real estate (refine boutique locations and outlet strategy), grow e-commerce and marketing.
- Industry: 2016–17 US mall traffic was declining. Many teen/young-women apparel chains were reporting negative comps and store closures. Online competitors and fast fashion are gaining share. Specialty retail valuations are compressed sector-wide.
- Auditor: a Big-4 firm. Clean opinion, and ICFR effective.
