## 3. Business and context known before T
- Revenue mix: mostly bulk/B2B (ghee, milk powder, butter sold to institutions, other FMCG/food companies, traders and exporters). The branded consumer business is a minority of revenue, and management is building it with new plants and distribution in the northern metros.
- Management narrative (FY16–FY17): shift towards higher-margin value-added and consumer products, expand milk procurement and chilling network, grow exports, deleverage over time.
- Industry context: Indian dairy is growing ~6–8% a year in value. Listed dairy peers trade at high P/Es on a consumer/brand narrative. Cooperatives dominate branded liquid milk. Bulk commodity products (milk powder, butter) are cyclical with global prices; global milk-powder prices were depressed in 2015–16.
