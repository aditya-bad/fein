## 3. Business and context known before T
- Products come from a small number of outside suppliers; the largest accounts for the clear majority of purchases (supplier concentration is a disclosed risk factor).
- Management commentary (Y0 annual report, paraphrased): growth driven by recruiting of new consultants and social-media selling; expects to retain many of the consultants recruited during the pandemic; investing in warehouse capacity and fulfilment automation to handle the higher volume; inventory deliberately built up to avoid stock-outs as supply chains tighten.
- Sales-force businesses typically see high consultant churn; the year after a recruiting surge often shows a decline.
- Macro at T: economy reopening; consumer spending on goods shifting back to services; freight and shipping costs rising fast; interest rates still near zero.
- Auditor: small regional firm; clean opinion. No restatements known.
