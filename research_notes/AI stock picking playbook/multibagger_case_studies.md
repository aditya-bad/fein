## Q1. Radico Khaitan: what actually happened, and is the user's "UBL issue → 120 to 4600" story accurate?

### Takeaway
The price range is roughly right: about ₹50–190 adjusted in 2008–2016, and about ₹4,100–4,750 in 2026, which is 25–90x depending on the start point. The cause and the timing in the user's version are wrong. The stock was **dead money for about 8 years (2008–2016)**. The multibagger move began only in **2016–17** and was driven by **premiumisation, deleveraging and operating leverage**. No 2008 "UBL issue" caused it. "UBL" is United Breweries Ltd, the Kingfisher beer company, which is not Radico's spirits competitor. The user probably means the UB Group / United Spirits (USL) / Vijay Mallya saga, which ran 2012–2016 (Kingfisher Airlines collapse, then Diageo taking control of USL in 2013). I found no source showing investors bought Radico in 2008 on a UB thesis.

### Cited Findings
- Year-by-year adjusted price (high/low): 2008 H 191 / L 49.6; 2009 H 131 / L 51.2; 2010–2016 range about 76–186 (2014 low 75.95, 2016 low 83.55); 2017 close 294; 2018 close 400; 2020 close 457 (low 220 in the COVID crash); 2021 close 1,236; 2023 close 1,658; 2024 close 2,606; 2025 close 3,298 — [stockpricearchive](https://stockpricearchive.com/share-price-history-and-returns/radico/). The data has inconsistencies: the site says "no split", yet the 2005–06 figures look unadjusted.
- A 2006 stock split took face value from ₹10 to ₹2 — [Search summary citing screener/finology data](https://www.screener.in/company/RADICO/)
- Recent price about ₹4,135–4,196, 52-week range ₹2,500–4,748 — [screener.in](https://www.screener.in/company/RADICO/); [Sharekhan](https://www.sharekhan.com/stock/radico-khaitan-ltd). The sites conflict: stockpricearchive shows a 2026 YTD high of only ₹3,679 as of June 2026, so the ₹4,748 high presumably came after June 2026. Verify.
- Origin story: Radico's Shaw Wallace bottling contract expired in the mid-1990s. Lalit Khaitan approached United Spirits for a partnership and was refused, which he believed was because Vijay Mallya wanted a stake in Radico. Radico then launched its own brands. **8PM whisky launched 8 Aug 1998** — [StockEdge](https://blog.stockedge.com/stock-insights-radico-khaitan-ltd/). So the real "UB" link is the 1990s Mallya rebuff, which pushed Radico to build brands of its own.
- Magic Moments vodka launched in 2005 ([HDFC Sec, Sep 2017](https://www.hdfcsec.com/hsl.research.pdf/RadicoKhaitan-PickoftheWeek-11091720170911085911.pdf)) or 2006 ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/magazine/how-radico-khaitan-is-driving-the-premiumisation-wave-in-indias-competitive-alcohol-industry)). Sources conflict. It now holds about 60% of Indian premium vodka — [StockEdge](https://blog.stockedge.com/stock-insights-radico-khaitan-ltd/)
- Radico had a JV with Diageo (Diageo Radico Distilleries), which launched Masterstroke Deluxe in 2007 and was later called off — [search summary](https://www.screener.in/company/RADICO/); [Tradebrains](https://tradebrains.in/how-vijay-mallyas-united-spirits-sale-helped-diageo-dominate-indias-premium-liquor-market/)

The open offer failed — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/diageo-seals-deal-with-mallya-to-pick-up-53-4-112110900175_1.html); [Business Standard Jul 2013](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/diageo-gains-control-over-united-spirits-ups-stake-to-25-113070400973_1.html)
- In the early-2017 pre-rerating state, net debt was ₹742 cr and the company "has not performed well in the last two years due to increasing raw material costs" — [Angel Broking 3QFY17](https://www.angelone.in/get-html-report/AngelBrokingResearch_Radico%20Khaitan_3QFY2017RU_130217.html)
- Turnaround numbers: debt/equity fell from 0.77 (FY17) to 0.15 (FY21). Revenue rose from ₹1,680 cr to ₹2,418 cr (+44%) while PAT rose from ₹80 cr to ₹271 cr (+239%), operating leverage — [StockEdge](https://blog.stockedge.com/stock-insights-radico-khaitan-ltd/)
- FY26: consolidated PAT +74.6% to ₹602.5 cr. Prestige & Above brands are now more than half of operating revenue — [search summary of FY26 analyses](https://alphastreet.com/india/radico-khaitan-premiumization-drives-strong-growth-outlook). Net debt was ₹5.7 bn at FY25 — [BS broker note](https://bsmedia.business-standard.com/_media/bs/data/market-reports/equity-brokertips/2025-06/17506722950.95489700.pdf)
- Valuepickr's long-running thread "Radico khaitan: alcoholic child" has discussion going back years — [Valuepickr](https://forum.valuepickr.com/t/radico-khaitan-alcoholic-child/3062?page=4)

### Inferences
- **Decomposition (my estimate, approximate):** From the FY17 trough at about ₹110 to about ₹4,200 is roughly 38x in price. PAT grew from ₹80 cr to ₹602 cr, about 7.5x. Assuming about 13.3–13.4 crore shares **[UNVERIFIED share count]**, market cap went from about ₹1,500 cr to about ₹56,000 cr. That implies trailing P/E rerated from about 18–19x to about 90x+, roughly a 5x multiple expansion. **About 7.5x earnings × about 5x rerating ≈ 38x.** This is the classic "twin engine" (Mayer) or "double whammy" pattern: a low-multiple, levered, out-of-favour company becomes a high-multiple premium consumer brand.
- **The signal that existed early (2016–17):** (a) Prestige & Above volumes growing while regular brands stagnated; (b) management guiding to debt reduction with no new big capex; (c) a raw-material (ENA/grain) cost downcycle reversing; (d) the USL/Diageo turmoil shifting the leader from volume to margin and cutting promotions and leaving shelf space for challengers **[UNVERIFIED as a documented contemporaneous thesis]**; (e) new luxury launches (Rampur single malt, about 2016; Jaisalmer gin; Royal Ranthambore whisky, about 2020) **[launch years UNVERIFIED]**.
- **What a 2008 buyer actually experienced:** a fall from ₹191 to ₹50 in 2008 (−74%), then 8 years range-bound at ₹75–190 before the move. Even a correct thesis required extreme patience and tolerance for opportunity cost. A good lesson in timing versus thesis.
- A user correction is needed: "120 → 4600" is achievable on a 2010–2016 entry, but the trigger was a **2016–17 business inflection (deleveraging + premium mix)** and not a 2008 competitor event.

## Q2. NVIDIA: who saw it early, which signals existed, and was the move earnings or multiple?

### Takeaway
NVIDIA's data-center revenue was visible and compounding years before ChatGPT: $339M in FY16, $1.93B in FY18 and $10.6B in FY22. After 2023, most of the stock's gain came from **earnings growth, not multiple expansion**. Forward P/E actually fell from above 40 to the high teens or low 20s by 2026 as earnings exploded. Holders had to sit through **two drawdowns of 50%+** (2018 and 2022).

### Cited Findings
- Annual data-center revenue: about $339M (FY ending Jan 2016), $830M (FY17), $1.93B (FY18), $2.93B (FY19), $2.98B (FY20), $6.70B (FY21), $10.61B (FY22) — [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/metrics/data-center-revenue/); [NVIDIA Oct 2022 investor presentation](https://s201.q4cdn.com/141608511/files/doc_presentations/2022/NVIDIA-Investor-Presentation-Oct-2022-(1).pdf). Quarterly data-center revenue later reached about $47.5B (cited for a quarter ending Jan 2024 in one table; this conflicts with my understanding that Q4 FY24 was about $18.4B, so it likely refers to a later quarter and needs checking) — [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/stocks/nvda/kpis/data-center-revenue-breakdown)
- Two drawdowns of 50% or more in a decade: 2018 (crypto-mining bust, rates, China tensions) and 2022 (tech selloff, crypto demand drying up) — [Yahoo Finance/Motley Fool](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-down-27-peak-history-100700214.html)
- After its breakout, the forward P/E spent years oscillating at levels frequently above 40 — [Motley Fool, Sep 2026](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/09/30/nvidia-stock-just-did-something-for-the-first-time/). Forward P/E fell from about 40 in mid-2025 to about 23 in July 2026 — [BigGo summary](https://finance.biggo.com/news/644cc7df-d580-48f0-8003-f034f109afa7). It was about 17x on 5 Oct 2026 (Bloomberg) and 15.4x on 6 Nov 2026 (TIKR, down from 30.1x) — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidia-nvda-more-doubled-revenue-132539113.html); [shattered.io](https://shattered.io/nvidia-17x-forward-earnings-cheap-multiple-half-story-2026/)
- 85% revenue growth against a 25% one-year stock gain explains the forward-P/E compression. The recovery has been "driven primarily by earnings growth rather than renewed multiple expansion" — [Mavengity](https://mavengity.com/articles/finance/nvidia-valuation-duration-not-existence/); [TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/nvidias-p-e-ratio-current-levels-historical-trends-and-outlook). Bear point: if 70% expected EPS growth turns out to be 40%, "17x" was never 17x — [shattered.io](https://shattered.io/nvidia-17x-forward-earnings-cheap-multiple-half-story-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Signal chain (the dates are well known but I did not re-source them this session; [UNVERIFIED])**: CUDA launched in 2006–07 (years of "wasted" R&D on GPGPU, criticised by investors at the time). AlexNet in 2012 was trained on two NVIDIA GTX 580 GPUs. Data-center revenue more than doubled in FY17 and FY18 (sourced above). Hyperscaler capex accelerated after ChatGPT (Nov 2022). In May 2023, Q2 FY24 revenue guidance of about $11B against about $7B consensus was the "unmistakable" public signal. The stock then multiplied several times *after* that public signal.
- **Second-order lesson:** the 2016–2018 investor who read data-center growth as an AI "picks and shovels" play was right but suffered a 50%+ drawdown in 2018 when crypto-driven gaming demand collapsed. Mixing the thesis segment (data center) with a cyclical segment (gaming/crypto) creates noisy drawdowns.
- Investors known to have held early include Baillie Gifford (Scottish Mortgage) and Cathie Wood's ARK, which sold much of its position in early 2023 before the run **[UNVERIFIED in this session]**. Selling too early is a recurring failure mode.
- After 2023 the move fits the "earnings engine only" pattern, unlike Radico's "twin engine". Very large earnings beats can produce multibagger returns even as the multiple compresses.

## Q3. Other Indian and US cases (signal → insight → numbers)

### Takeaway
The best-documented Indian multibaggers fit a few templates. **Turnaround under new management** (Eicher/Royal Enfield). **Policy-shift rerating** (defence PSUs 2020–24, where the move was largely multiple expansion ahead of earnings). **Supply-chain shift** (Deepak Nitrite, China+1). **PLI-led scale** (Dixon). Many of the most recent "fastest wealth creators" were cyclical or policy beneficiaries.

### Cited Findings
**Eicher Motors (Royal Enfield)**
- Siddhartha Lal joined and became Royal Enfield CEO around 2000. The brand was "barely trudging along" with stagnant sales before the product reboot (repositioned controls, more powerful models) — [Business Standard](https://business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/ceo-of-the-year-racing-ahead-117031700863_1.html); [Medium/Finception](https://medium.com/finception/eicher-motors-from-good-to-great-69bab6ca47a2)
- Eicher was the Fastest Wealth Creator in Motilal Oswal's 19th study (2009–14). Eicher, Supreme Industries and Page Industries were in the top 10 fastest — [MOSL WC study 2009-14](https://www.motilaloswal.com/content/dam/mofsl-website-adobe/investor-relations/wealth-creation/wc19.pdf)
- From April 2005 at about ₹32 adjusted to about ₹2,754, an 86x gain, by the time of PrimeInvestor's article — [PrimeInvestor, "A multi-bagger that no one foresaw"](https://primeinvestor.in/?p=56537). Recent price about ₹7,630 (7 Sep 2026; 52-week range ₹6,411–8,230) — [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/stocks/eicher-motors-ltd/). ### Inferences
- **Defence is the cleanest "policy → rerating" case.** In the early phase, multiples doubled before earnings caught up. That means future returns depend on order-book execution, which explains why 2025–26 coverage shows the sector diverging (HAL/BEL holding up while shipbuilders depend on subsidy support — [Whalesbook](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/aerospace-defense/Defence-Stocks-Diverge-HAL-BEL-Lead-as-Shipbuilders-Eye-Subsidized-Growth/69942e9acffb93b3e927e0b7)).

- Cases not researched with sources this session: Bajaj Finance, Page, Astral, Tata Elxsi, railway PSUs, Polycab, Avanti Feeds, Netflix, Tesla, Apple, Super Micro and Palantir. All are well-known multibaggers, but treat any numbers for them as unverified until sourced.

## Q4. How professionals "read between the lines": the process

### Takeaway
Across the cases, the early signal was almost never secret information. It was **publicly visible data that most investors dismissed** because it sat inside a declining or boring narrative. Examples: segment revenue (NVIDIA data center), mix shift (Radico's Prestige & Above share), a new CEO's product reset (Royal Enfield), a policy document (defence indigenisation, PLI), or a capex for import substitution (Deepak phenol). The skill is to follow the second-order effect to where the market has not yet priced it.

### Cited Findings
- Chris Mayer's 100-bagger traits: high returns on capital (ROE of 15%+ in most years), a durable competitive advantage, a focus on ROIC and FCF, avoiding heavy debt so that drawdowns don't cause permanent impairment, and very long holding periods (most took 20–25 years; one summary says about 17 years at about 26% CAGR) — [Hedge Fund Alpha](https://hedgefundalpha.com/strategies/christopher-mayers-notes-100-baggers/); [TIP](https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/articles/100-bagger-stocks/); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/hardbound/bagging-rights-2772)
- Small starting size: 8 of the top-10 Indian fastest creators (2016–21) started below ₹2,000 cr market cap — [Business Standard / MOSL](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/markets/reliance-industries-largest-wealth-creator-in-2016-21-says-motilal-oswal-121121401678_1.html)
- MOSL's 2024 study theme, "Creating Wealth Through Bruised Blue Chips", buys quality companies during temporary distress — [InvestmentGuru](https://investmentguruindia.com/newsdetail/motilal-oswal-29-th-annual-wealth-creation-study-2024-creating-wealth-through-bruised-blue-chips555308)

### Inferences (a synthesised checklist drawn from the cases)
1. **Segment, not consolidated, data.** Find the small, fast-growing segment hidden inside a big, flat company. Examples: NVIDIA data center in FY16–18, Radico Prestige & Above, Royal Enfield inside Eicher's truck-heavy group.
2. **Mix shift → margin → operating leverage.** Revenue +44% turned into PAT +239% at Radico (FY17–21). Watch gross margin and A&P spend in concall commentary.
3. **Balance-sheet inflection.** Deleveraging (Radico D/E 0.77 → 0.15) both raises equity value and triggers the rerating.
4. **Policy documents as order-book precursors.** Defence indigenisation, PLI and China+1 come first. Map first-order beneficiaries (OEMs: HAL, BEL, Dixon), then second-order (component/subsystem suppliers), then third-order (raw materials, testing, logistics).
5. **Competitor distress / industry structure change.** When a leader is distracted (USL under Mallya's debt and then the Diageo transition; wind OEM consolidation after Suzlon's collapse), challengers can gain share. Verify with channel checks (retail shelves, dealer waiting periods such as Royal Enfield's months-long waiting lists **[UNVERIFIED]**).
6. **Read the concall for capacity utilisation and capex language.** A company at more than 80% utilisation announcing a brownfield expansion is the precursor to an earnings step-up (Deepak's phenol plant, Dixon's mobile lines).
7. **Insider/promoter behaviour.** Promoter buying and no pledging. Radico's promoter refusing to sell a stake to Mallya is the anecdotal form.
8. **Valuation starting point.** The largest returns (Radico, defence) had both engines: earnings 5–8x and P/E 2–5x. NVIDIA after 2023 shows that earnings alone can carry the move, but buying at already-high multiples removes one engine and adds derating risk.

- No primary concall transcripts or Valuepickr posts were retrieved that show a specific early call (for example, a 2016 Valuepickr post on Radico's premiumisation).

## Q5. Base rates, drawdowns and survivorship bias (Bessembinder + failure cases)

### Takeaway
Multibaggers are rare and violent. In the US, **4% of stocks explain all net wealth creation since 1926** and the majority underperform T-bills. Globally, about **2.4%** of firms create all net wealth. Almost every big winner had a 50–95% drawdown along the way. The same patterns (policy tailwind, sector boom, turnaround) produced spectacular failures, such as Suzlon's 99.6% wipeout.

### Cited Findings
- US, CRSP 1926–2016: the majority of common stocks have lifetime buy-and-hold returns below one-month T-bills. About 58% fail to beat T-bills. The top 4% explain the entire net gain of the US market — [Bessembinder, JFE 2018 (ASU PDF)](https://wpcarey.asu.edu/sites/default/files/2021-10/do-stocks-outperform-treasury-bills.pdf); [ASU news](https://news.wpcarey.asu.edu/20180917-do-stocks-outperform-treasury-bills)
- Global, 1990–2020: 63,785 firms in 43 countries. The top 1,526 firms (2.39%) created 100% of net shareholder wealth. The study was funded by Baillie Gifford, and the figures come from a presentation reported by Morningstar — [Morningstar AU](https://www.morningstar.com.au/stocks/only-2-4-of-companies-deliver-all-net-shareholder-wealth)
- Drawdowns: Berkshire, the best performer in Mayer's study, was cut in half at least three times — [TIP](https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/articles/100-bagger-stocks/). Amazon fell 94% (1999–2001) and took about 10 years to regain its peak — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2016/06/26/amazon-stocks-history-the-importance-of-patience.aspx). NVIDIA had two 50%+ drawdowns (2018, 2022) — [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-down-27-peak-history-100700214.html). Radico fell 74% in 2008 and then went sideways for 8 years — [stockpricearchive](https://stockpricearchive.com/share-price-history-and-returns/radico/)
- Survivorship caveat on Amazon: the many stocks that fell this far and never recovered aren't on anyone's chart — [ChartRow](https://chartrow.com/quote/amzn/drawdown)

**Failure / counterpoint cases**
- **Suzlon:** the all-time high of ₹430.92 (adjusted, 9 Jan 2008) fell to an all-time low of ₹1.51 (26 Mar 2020), about −99.6%. The stock fell 77%+ in 2008 alone. The causes were an over-ambitious, debt-funded expansion (Hansen, REpower acquisitions **[acquisition names UNVERIFIED]**) and share loss to Gamesa, GE, Enercon and Vestas. It rebounded about 42x from the 2020 low by Nov 2024, but 2008 buyers remain far underwater (about ₹55 in Jun 2026) — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/company/13872); [Samco](https://www.samco.in/knowledge-center/articles/why-suzlon-share-price-is-going-down/); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/suzlon-energy-shares-down-96-in-5-years-this-stock-rebounded-42x-heres-why-456906-2024-12-11). Same "green energy policy tailwind" pattern; failed because of leverage.
- **Deepak Nitrite:** the China+1 winner of 2020–21 had falling FY26 profit and a price well below its peak — [Upstox](https://upstox.com/stocks/deepak-nitrite-ltd-share-price/INE288B01029) (price unverified). A correct thesis meets cyclical spread compression.
- **Defence:** a rerating ahead of earnings (BS 2023) and multiples about 2x historical averages in 2025 leave the stocks vulnerable to a de-rating. Some outlets reported defence shares "continuing to tumble" in a later correction — [Equentis](https://www.equentis.com/blog/from-bel-to-mazdock-defence-shares-continue-to-tumble-even-as-stock-markets-rebound-heres-why/)
- **Amazon peers (dot-com):** QQQ fell 63.7% over the same window, and most dot-com names never recovered — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2016/06/26/amazon-stocks-history-the-importance-of-patience.aspx)

### Inferences
