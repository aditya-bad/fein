**Rules used.** "Confirmed" means a primary document (exchange filing, SEC 8-K, RBI release), or two independent reputable outlets.
"Partly" means the core event is real but the headline's framing or a detail is not supported. "Unconfirmed" means we could not find
a primary document or a second outlet. "False/stale" means the primary record contradicts the headline or shows it is out of date.
**No paper call, probability or date was changed.** The "bears on" column gives a direction only.
Research, not SEBI-registered advice.

## What this changes
- **Nothing in the paper calls.** All calls keep their original probability and date, so the Brier scores test what we actually said.
- **NVIDIA (US-NVDA)**: the existing record `event:US-NVDA:ceo-resignation-2026-11` now has its sequel, the reinstatement (new event record). Two watch items:
  the Q3 FY27 10-Q (any disclosure of the SEC or California AG matters), and the independent committee's findings.
- **UTTAMSUGAR**: the L45 price calendar has been started (`research_notes/Deep dives/UTTAMSUGAR_price_calendar.md`). The next dated decision is the SC hearing on 2-Dec-2026.
  The UP SAP is still the largest open variable.
- **Live dives with an addendum**: UTTAMSUGAR (#8–#13) and UTKARSHBNK (#14–#15). INDRAMEDCO and SUNFLAG had nothing material confirmed.
- **Process lessons for the news layer**: (a) Google News re-surfaces stale items (Polycab "reserves order", the 2025 ICEMA safeguard plea).
  Before flagging, check the earliest-dated copy and the company's own filings. (b) Headline verbs overstate ("DOJ closes investigation" was an HSR
  expiry; "ousted" was a resignation accepted under pressure; "accounting practices" was a disclosure probe). (c) The RBI press-release
  archive (`BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=N`, sequential) is a cheap primary check for RBI meeting headlines.
