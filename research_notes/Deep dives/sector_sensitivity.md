# Sector-sensitivity checklist (companion to `deep_dive_protocol.md` v1.5)

**Version 1 · 2026-11-26.** Stage 4 (deep dive), read at rule zero (v1.5 mandatory line 3). It unlocks L4 sensitivities that start
from the right external inputs, and a dated macro context for stage 5. Research, not SEBI-registered advice.

**How to use it.** Find the subject's `sector` in `research_notes/Backtests/india_pead_live/industry_map.csv` (22 values; counts in
brackets are names in the map). Copy its drivers and must-checks into the dive. For each **snapshot field**, write the value and
date from `research_notes/Macro/snapshot_YYYY-MM.json` (`tools/macro/snapshot.py`). Each driver then needs a model line or a bps
sensitivity in L4, or "not material" with a reason. Conglomerates and multi-segment firms use one row per segment.

**Tags.** `[E: …]` = evidence from our own dives, lessons or sector playbooks (memdb `theme:sector-*`, 2026-11-19; the
playbooks are **single-source**). `[S]` = standard industry knowledge, not yet tested by us. Treat `[S]` as a question to
answer, not a finding.

**Snapshot fields available** (monthly): `rbi_repo_rate`, `india_10y_gsec`, `india_cpi_yoy` (manual), `usd_inr`, `dxy`,
`brent_usd`, `gold_usd_oz`, `gold_inr_10g`, `nifty50_close/pe/pb/div_yield`, `nifty50_earnings_yield`,
`nifty_ey_minus_gsec10` (context only, L51), `us_10y`, `fpi_net_equity_month` (manual).
**Not in the snapshot (read the external series by hand):** monsoon/IMD rainfall, steel/coal/iron-ore/LME metals, cotton,
palm oil, cane SAP/FRP, gas APM price, freight indices, US tariffs. Gold sources disagreed in Nov-2026 (L51): cross-check both.
