## Sensitivities (from the live dive, unchanged)
- +₹10/qtl UP SAP ≈ +₹100/t cane ≈ **₹30–39 cr EBITDA (15–19% of TTM)** at 3.0–3.9 mt crush.
- The SAP alone costs about ₹4,150 per qtl of sugar at 9.42% recovery, which is roughly the FY26 realisation (₹4,143). So the MSP and ex-mill price set the spread.
