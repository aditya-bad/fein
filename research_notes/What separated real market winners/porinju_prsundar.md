# Porinju Veliyath and P R Sundar: Real Track Records, Methods, Edges and Controversies (as of Nov 2026)

## Porinju Veliyath: career, method, PMS track record (incl. 2018–2020 crash), the "Porinju effect", SEBI matters

### Takeaway
Veliyath's audited-style PMS series (from his firm's own site) shows a genuine long-run edge in small caps, including big wins in FY14–FY17 and FY21. It also shows a catastrophic two-year drawdown: FY19 -31.87% and FY20 -51.48%, about -67% compounded, while the Nifty 50 TRI was roughly -13% over the same two years. That loss was partly self-admitted ("chor" stock bets, the LEEL Electricals misjudgement). An investor who entered at the FY18 peak was roughly back to breakeven only by end-FY22. I found no SEBI enforcement order against him for front-running or manipulation; the only SEBI order naming him is a 2009 consent (settlement) order in a Geojit Securities matter whose terms I could not read.

### Inferences
- Compounding the firm's own figures, FY19 and FY20 together give about -66.9%, against about -12.7% for the Nifty 50 TRI. An FY18-end investor was at about 0.83x after FY21 and about 1.03x after FY22, so roughly four years with no gain. The Nifty 50 TRI went to about 1.81x over the same four years. A long-run 22% vs 18% CAGR edge therefore hides a period that would have broken most retail holders. Redemptions near the bottom also mean the dollar-weighted (investor) return is likely far below the time-weighted figure.
- Volatility is extreme in both directions: +152% in FY21 after -51% in FY20. That fits a concentrated (10–12 stock) micro/small-cap book with a high beta to small-cap liquidity cycles, plus stock-specific governance risk (LEEL).
- His edge seems to be early discovery of neglected small caps and turnarounds, plus a public following that adds price impact. That following is also a hazard for retail copycats: they buy after the bulk-deal disclosure and the move, often at worse prices, and they lack his information and exit timing.
- His business model is a fee-earning PMS/AIF (2% plus 10% over a 10% hurdle) built on a celebrity reputation, alongside proprietary investing. His media profile is a marketing asset for the PMS.

### Gaps
- I could not read the terms or allegations of the 2009 SEBI consent order. No front-running or disclosure-violation order against him was found; the "front-running/disclosure allegations" in the brief are unverified.
- There is no official dollar-weighted return, client count history, or AUM time series. FY26 return (to Mar 2026) was not found.
- I found no primary source for the "Smallcap Shankar" moniker, or for Cochin Shipyard as his pick.
- I did not verify the BEML 51% figure beyond a topic-page snippet. I did not access the original ET "chor stocks" interviews; the quotes come from blog reproductions.

## P R Sundar: options-selling method, claimed returns, advisory/course business, the SEBI order, and option-selling risk

### Takeaway
SEBI's settlement order of 25 May 2023 found that Sundar's Mansun Consultancy had collected about ₹4.6 crore in unregistered advisory fees, via daily Telegram trade calls sold on his blog. The order is from 2023, not 2024 as the brief assumed. Sundar, his wife and the company settled without admitting or denying the findings. They disgorged ₹6,07,69,863 (fees plus 12% interest), paid ₹15.6 lakh each (₹46.8 lakh total), and agreed to a one-year bar from dealing in securities. His method, selling Nifty/Bank Nifty and stock options for theta, earns steady premium with fat-tail losses. SEBI's F&O studies show about 91% of individual F&O traders lose money.

### Cited Findings
**Background and strategy**
- Secondary, inconsistent bios say he was born in Purisai, Tamil Nadu, in about 1964, has a postgraduate degree in mathematics, and taught math in Gujarat and then Singapore (1993–2005). They say he began trading in 2007 and specialised in selling OTM puts, later becoming known for options selling and theta capture. One source gives a conflicting 1982/engineer/Cognizant biography. Treat all of this as low-reliability — [ISFM](https://isfm.co.in/p-r-sundar-scam/); [WeRiseByLiftingOthers](https://www.werisebyliftingothers.in/2024/04/journey-of-trader-p-r-sundar.html)
- His YouTube channel has about 1.2 million subscribers, and he runs paid options workshops and an "Options Conclave" — [ISFM](https://isfm.co.in/p-r-sundar-net-worth/); [prsundar.com Options Conclave](http://prsundar.com/optionsconclave/)
- His own claims (X, Aug 2023): "I always discourage people with tiny capital doing Options Trading". **SEBI settlement order (primary source)** — [SEBI Settlement Order SO/AA/EFD2/2023-24/7081, 25 May 2023](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/may-2023/1685095933750.pdf)
- Applicants: Mansun Consultancy Pvt Ltd (incorporated 30 Jun 2017; the Sundars own 50% each), P R Sundar, and Mangayarkarasi Sundar.
- SEBI acted on two references alleging advisory without registration. - Terms: ₹15,60,000 each (₹46,80,000 total), plus disgorgement of ₹6,07,69,863 (fees plus ₹1,47,74,100 interest at 12% from 1 Jun 2020 to 2 Feb 2023). They must refrain from buying, selling or dealing in securities for one year from the order. The settlement was "without admitting or denying the findings of fact and conclusions of law".

### Inferences
- The verifiable money in the Sundar story is advisory and education revenue: about ₹4.6 crore in fees in roughly 2020–2022 from Telegram calls alone, before workshops and YouTube. His trading P&L is entirely self-reported, and no audited track record exists. Retail investors should treat claimed option-selling returns as unverified marketing.
- Short options (short straddles and strangles) have negative skew: frequent small gains and rare large losses, such as gap moves in March 2020, election-result days or budget days. His own "lost ₹15 lakh in a day" tweet shows the intraday tail even for a well-capitalised trader. Retail sellers with smaller capital and margins are more exposed to forced exits at the worst prices.
- SEBI's study did not break out option sellers in any source I found, so whether option sellers do better than buyers among individuals is unverified. The broad result (about 91% lose, with prop/algo desks taking the profits) suggests that copying a famous seller's style is not a reliable retail edge.

### Gaps
- No SEBI order in 2024 involving Sundar was found; the brief's "2024" appears to be an error for the 25 May 2023 order. Some media rounded the amounts to "₹6.5 crore penalty". The correct figures are ₹6.08 crore disgorgement plus ₹46.8 lakh settlement.
- No independently verified trading returns or COVID-period P&L for Sundar. His post-ban (2024–2026) business activity was not found in reliable news.
- SEBI's breakdown of losses for option sellers vs buyers among individuals was not sourced.
- His biography details (age, career) come from low-quality blogs and conflict with each other.

## The real business model of famous market personalities (fees vs trading profits)

### Takeaway
For both men, the most verifiable income comes from fees charged to followers, not their own trading. Veliyath's reputation funds a PMS/AIF charging 2% plus 10% above a 10% hurdle. Sundar's following funded advisory calls (₹4.6 crore over about two-plus years per SEBI) and workshops. SEBI enforcement since 2023 has targeted exactly this pattern: unregistered "education" that is really advice.

### Cited Findings
- Equity Intelligence charges a 2% p.a. fixed fee plus a 10% performance fee above a 10% p.a. hurdle; the minimum is ₹50 lakh — [Equity Intelligence](https://www.equityintelligence.com/portfolio-management-service)
- Mansun collected ₹4.60 crore in advisory fees for Telegram calls — [SEBI order 25 May 2023](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/may-2023/1685095933750.pdf). Sundar also sells workshops and conclaves — [prsundar.com](http://prsundar.com/optionsconclave/)
- SEBI has since acted against course-selling finfluencers, including "Baap of Chart" (₹17 crore refund) and Avadhut Sathe (over ₹546 crore impounded, Dec 2025) — [BQ Prime](https://www.bqprime.com/law-and-policy/sebi-bars-baap-of-chart-finfluencer-orders-rs-17-crore-refund); [JusCorpus](https://www.juscorpus.com/educators-or-unlicensed-advisers/)

### Inferences
- A 2% fixed fee on about ₹800–1,400 crore of AUM (unverified range) would be roughly ₹16–29 crore a year before performance fees, earned in down years too. In FY19–FY20 clients lost about two-thirds while the fixed fee still accrued, which shows how fee income and client outcomes can diverge.
- Public visibility (TV, X, YouTube) is a customer-acquisition engine. Retail followers rarely see dollar-weighted outcomes, drawdowns or regulatory history in the "highlight reel".

### Gaps
- No disclosed fee revenue for Equity Intelligence (the company financials were not checked). No disclosed workshop revenue or personal trading P&L for Sundar.
