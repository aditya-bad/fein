# India Customs Duty on Gold (July 2024 – 28 November 2026)

> **Verification & Tooling Limitation Notice (Partially Blocked)**
>
> This task required using only built-in web search and web page reading tools (with no shell/terminal commands), citing **real, direct primary-source URLs** (`cbic.gov.in`, `egazette.gov.in`, `pib.gov.in`, `indiabudget.gov.in`) without redirect links, and saving one text file per source page containing the real URL and verbatim copied passages.
>
> In this environment:
> 1. **No built-in web page reading / URL-fetching tool is available** (only `search_web` is available, and shell/terminal commands are prohibited).
> 2. `search_web` returns synthesized search summaries rather than raw page/PDF text, and every source link it exposes is a `vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/...` proxy redirect that cannot be resolved without fetching it.
>
> Accordingly:
> - **No per-source verbatim `.txt` files have been written**, because no primary source page or PDF could be fetched directly to verify its live URL or copy passages verbatim.
> - **No redirect links and no unverified/guessed direct document URLs are included below.**
> - The table and notes below summarize what was found via `search_web` (including conflicting secondary reports where found) and must be verified directly against the official Gazette/CBIC PDFs once a web page reading tool is available.

## Primary Sources Identified (Direct URLs Unverified Due to Missing Web-Page Reader Tool)

Because this environment lacks a built-in web page reading tool and prohibits terminal commands, the `vertexaisearch` redirect links returned by `search_web` could not be resolved to live primary-source URLs, nor could the official PDFs/pages be opened to extract verified verbatim passages. The primary official instruments identified via search are:

1. **CBIC Notification No. 30/2024-Customs** (`G.S.R. 433(E)`), dated 23 July 2024 (effective 24 July 2024) — Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), amending Notification No. 50/2017-Customs (BCD on gold reduced to 5%). Hosted on CBIC Tax Information Portal (`taxinformation.cbic.gov.in`), e-Gazette (`egazette.gov.in`), and Union Budget 2024–25 portal (`indiabudget.gov.in`).
2. **CBIC Notification No. 32/2024-Customs** (`G.S.R. 435(E)`), dated 23 July 2024 (effective 24 July 2024) — Ministry of Finance (Department of Revenue), amending Notification No. 11/2021-Customs (AIDC on gold bars reduced to 1% and gold doré to 0.35%). Hosted on `taxinformation.cbic.gov.in`, `egazette.gov.in`, and `indiabudget.gov.in`.
3. **CBIC Notifications No. 31/2024-Customs and No. 33/2024-Customs**, dated 23 July 2024 (effective 24 July 2024) — companion amendments to India–UAE CEPA Notification No. 22/2022-Customs and Notification No. 57/2000-Customs. Hosted on `taxinformation.cbic.gov.in`, `egazette.gov.in`, and `indiabudget.gov.in`.
4. **PIB Press Release (Union Budget 2024–25)**, dated 23 July 2024 — announcing reduction of customs duties on gold and silver from 15% to 6%. Hosted on `pib.gov.in` (exact `PRID` URL not retrievable without a page-reading tool).
5. **CBIC Notification No. 45/2025-Customs** (`G.S.R. 781(E)`), dated 24 October 2025 (effective 1 November 2025), and companion **Notification No. 44/2025-Customs** — consolidating Notification No. 50/2017-Customs (retaining 5% BCD on gold under Table I S. Nos. 192, 194, 196). Hosted on `taxinformation.cbic.gov.in` and `egazette.gov.in`.
6. **CBIC Notification No. 15/2026-Customs** (`G.S.R. 358(E)`), dated 12 May 2026 (effective 13 May 2026) — amending Table I (S. Nos. 192–197) of Notification No. 45/2025-Customs to raise BCD on gold from 5% to 10%. Hosted on `taxinformation.cbic.gov.in` and `egazette.gov.in`.
7. **CBIC Notification No. 16/2026-Customs** (`G.S.R. 359(E)`), dated 12 May 2026 (effective 13 May 2026) — amending Notification No. 11/2018-Customs (SWS) and Notification No. 11/2021-Customs (Sl. Nos. 15A–15D, raising AIDC on gold bars to 5% and gold doré to 4.35%). Hosted on `taxinformation.cbic.gov.in` and `egazette.gov.in`.
8. **CBIC Notifications No. 17/2026-Customs and No. 18/2026-Customs**, dated 12 May 2026 (effective 13 May 2026) — companion amendments to Notification No. 57/2000-Customs and India–UAE CEPA Notification No. 22/2022-Customs. Hosted on `taxinformation.cbic.gov.in` and `egazette.gov.in`.

## Why Per-Source Verbatim `.txt` Files Were Not Created

Per the instructions, each source `.txt` file must contain a **real (non-redirect) primary-source URL** on line 1 followed by the **relevant passage copied verbatim** from that source page. Because:
- no built-in web page reading tool is available in this session,
- shell/terminal commands are disallowed, and
- `search_web` only returns AI-generated summaries with `vertexaisearch` redirect links,

creating per-source `.txt` files from `search_web` summaries would require either inserting unverified/guessed URLs or presenting non-verbatim summaries as verbatim primary text. Rather than fabricating evidence, this limitation is reported transparently.
