Part 1 · The setup
Macro thesis · checked against dated sources

Each thesis held up, but every one needed a correction.

The brief going in had stale or overstated premises in all four tracks. Below is what is actually true as of mid-November 2026, and what changed in the picking because of it.

Track 01 · Rupee

The rupee is weak, not collapsing.

Still ~6–7% weaker YoY (₹88–89 in late 2025). US tariff on India cut from 50% to 18% (Feb 2026); IEEPA tariffs struck down 20 Feb 2026, refunds flowing. India's MSCI EM weight is ~11–12%, down from ~21%, so exporters are under-owned.

CorrectionThe "₹96–97" premise is stale. Record low ~₹96.5–97.2 was in May 2026; by 13 Nov it had recovered to ~₹94.2. Freight is 2.5–3x normal and mostly not passed through, so bulky low-margin exporters (textiles, shrimp) lose the gain.
Track 02 · Power & DC

Demand is real. The shortage trade is ending.

~1.8 GW of DC capacity in H1 2026, forecasts of 6–8 GW by 2030. Record peak demand 270.8 GW (21 May 2026). ICRA sees ₹5.6 trn transmission capex FY27–32. BESS tenders 100+ GWh, but only ~9 GWh operational.

CorrectionTransformer makers are showing peak margins: Shilchar, Voltamp, Indo Tech and Vilas have printed 3–4 quarters of falling OPM while everyone adds capacity. Value moved to towers and T&D EPC at 14–24x with 1.8–1.9x order cover.
Track 03 · Consumption & savings

Flows are high but flat; volumes came late.

SIP record ₹32,490 cr in Sep 2026; Oct ₹32,160 cr (−1% MoM), equity MF inflows −14% MoM. GST 2.0 volumes showed up with a lag, in FY27 prints. RBI cut 125 bp in 2025, then held at 5.25% all of 2026.

CorrectionSIP flows are flattening, so own AUM-stock earners, not flow momentum. The "F&O losers" short is stale: Angel One and CDSL Sep-26 quarter profits already recovered after the STT hike. No further RBI easing; inflation risk points up.
Track 04 · Quant fingerprint

The pattern is rare, and often a mirage.

Nine screens (debt reduction, growth without dilution, capacity expansion, FII buying, quarterly growers, Piotroski, magic formula and others) gave ~165 overlap candidates in the ₹300–10,000 cr band. Only 4 survived.

CorrectionSeveral perfect-looking fingerprints died on a single check: Syncom has the numbers but no identifiable cause; Jayaswal Neco deleveraged beautifully with 99.9% promoter pledge. Two survivors (Gulshan, Birla Cable) are commodity spreads, not franchises.

Why a basket: the honest arithmetic
Five equal positions, one plausible 3–5 year outcome.
1 name triples ............ 3.00x
1 name does well .......... 1.50x
1 name goes nowhere ....... 1.00x
2 names hit bear case ..... 0.65x each
Basket ≈ 1.36x in 4 yrs ≈ 8% a year

Even with a genuine winner, that is roughly index-like. Most picks will not be multibaggers; several bear cases in the deep-dives are −29% to −36%. The satellite is a bet on skew, sized so a bad outcome costs a few percent of the portfolio, not the plan.
