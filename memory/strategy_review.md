# Strategy review — where the edge is (and isn't), 23 Nov 2026

Written after 7 pre-registered signal tests, 3 stage-2 filter tests, 8 credibility ledgers across 3 sectors,
5 deep dives and the full pipeline build. Re-read before choosing new work. Update after each results season.

## 2. The strategic conclusion
1. **Mechanical edges in public data are mostly arbitraged away** (McLean–Pontiff confirmed in our own tests).
   Our durable, cheap advantage is on the **defensive side**: avoid stocks after big earnings misses; avoid or size
   small high-pledge / insolvent-looking firms. Avoiding blow-ups compounds as surely as finding winners.
2. **Offensive edge must come from depth, not rules**: reading what others skip (filings, footnotes, concalls
   across years), management credibility over time, and the user's ground checks. These are expensive for humans
   and cheap for us — that asymmetry is the real edge thesis ("Lynch at scale").
3. **Proof comes only from forward scoring.** 115 dated calls resolve Jan–Jul 2027. Until then: no idea-pitching.

## 3. What to do next (priority order)
1. **Stop adding new mechanical signal tests** unless a strong, specific reason exists (e.g., an India-specific
   information that is hard to process, not a published US anomaly).
2. **Shift effort to the offensive engine:**
   a. Promise tracking at scale on the full universe once the Pi/Kite/Screener arrive (automated pipeline exists).
   b. Deep dives using protocol v1.3 on companies that pass the defensive filters, producing decision cards and
      forward calls (stage 4→7), sector by sector, India-weighted.
   c. A "variant perception" log: for each deep dive, what the market believes vs what we believe, scored later.
3. **Keep the defensive layer running nightly** (detectors, solvency screen, PEAD live tracker) on the Pi.
4. **Prepare the scoring season** (Jan–Feb 2027): resolver dry runs, review queue, post-mortems per method.
5. **Start phase A (thinking system for the user) as a draft** only after the first scoring season shows which
   methods earn their place.
