# Knowledge base — distilled research findings

Last updated: 2026-11-19. Full sourced detail: `reports/` and `research_notes/`.

## Base rates & reality
- Bessembinder: ~58% of US stocks (1926–2016) lost to T-bills; 4.3% (1,092 firms)
  created all $35T net wealth. Global 1990–2020: ~2.4% of 63,785 firms.
- Motilal Oswal 100x study: ~47 of ~3,500 stocks did 100x, ~12 yrs avg.
- SEBI F&O: individuals losing — FY22 89%; FY22–24 93% (₹1.8 lakh cr); FY25 91%
  (net ₹1.06–1.12 lakh cr); FY26 87.7% (net ₹91,685 cr; 82.1% lose even before costs).
  FY26 prop gross profit ₹44,483 cr, FPI ₹13,896 cr; 99% of it to algo users; top-10
  prop firms ≈ 75% of prop profit. Individuals pay ~₹25k cr/yr in costs.
- Jane Street: SEBI interim order 3 Jul 2025, ₹4,843.5 cr impounded, alleged ₹36,502 cr
  net India profit (Jan 2023–Mar 2025); SAT order reserved 21 Oct 2026, no final ruling.
- Day traders: Brazil 97% of persistent ones lose; Taiwan <1% reliably profitable.
- Behaviour gap: Morningstar 2025 1.2 pts/yr; India (Axis MF) funds 19.1% vs investors 13.8%.
- Kaplan & Rauh: 69% of Forbes 400 started businesses. Founders > investors on rich lists.
- Fidelity "best investors were dead/forgot" study = **myth**. "Most Magellan investors
  lost money" = **myth** (Fidelity denied); real fact is a timing gap.
- Proving 2% alpha statistically takes ~16–64 years of data.

## Legends — what really happened
- **Buffett:** Partnership 1957–69 ~23.8%/yr net vs Dow 7.4% (no mgmt fee, 25% over 6%).
  Buffett's Alpha paper: Sharpe 0.79, ~1.7x leverage, float cost ~1.72%; alpha
  insignificant after BAB + QMJ factors. ~99% of wealth after 60–65. 1951 return
  75.8% (not "144%"). Korea picks 2004–05 (not 1999). Mistakes: textiles, Dexter, airlines.
- **Lynch:** Magellan 1977–90 ~29%/yr; $20M → $14B; 1,400 stocks by 1989; turnover ~300%;
  200+ visits, ~700 annual reports/yr; quit at 46.
- **Damani:** 1992 bear vs Harshad Mehta; long holds (VST, HDFC Bank IPO); D-Mart 2002
  (EDLP, owned stores, pay suppliers fast); ~90% wealth = D-Mart stake at IPO.
  "Bear cartel / week from bankruptcy" stories unsourced.
- **Jhunjhunwala:** "₹5,000 start" but borrowed client money; Tata Tea 1986 (₹43→143,
  cost ~₹2.15 lakh); biggest wealth from 1992 bear trade; Titan bought ~₹30–50 (₹3 is
  split-adjusted); A2Z −94% loss; Aptech SEBI settlement 14 Jul 2021 (~₹18.5 cr him; ~₹37 cr all parties).
- **Porinju:** Equity Intelligence PMS: FY19 −31.87%, FY20 −51.48% (≈ −67% vs Nifty −13%);
  "chor stocks" admission; LEEL loss. No front-running order found.
- **PR Sundar:** SEBI settlement 25 May 2023 (unregistered advisory; repaid ₹6.08 cr;
  1-yr bar). Returns self-reported only.

## Case studies
- **Radico Khaitan:** UBL = United Breweries (beer), not a rival. Fell 74% in 2008
  (₹191→49.6), sideways ₹76–186 until 2016. Move driven 2016–17+: premiumisation,
  D/E 0.77→0.15 (FY17–21), revenue +44% vs PAT +239%. Real UB link: 1990s Mallya refusal
  → Radico built 8PM (1998). No source for a 2008 UB thesis.
- **NVIDIA:** DC revenue $339M (FY16) → $10.6B (FY22) pre-ChatGPT; two >50% drawdowns;
  post-2023 gains from earnings, forward P/E fell ~40 → ~15–17x by late 2026.
- Eicher 86x (2005→); Amazon −94% 2000–01; Suzlon −99.6% (debt); defence PSUs = rerating.
