## Mission
Build a compounding investing system that gives the user a genuine, durable edge in
Indian small/mid caps (later US), so a few big winners pay for many small losers and
nothing ever wipes us out.

## Core truth (from research)
Every legend had a structural edge, not just knowledge:
- Buffett: tiny capital in an unscreened market; read Moody's manuals page by page;
  later ~1.7x leverage via insurance float cheaper than T-bills; never forced to sell.
- Lynch: 200+ company visits & ~700 annual reports/yr; pre-Reg FD private access.
- Damani: broking-family networks; 1992 short side; ~90% of wealth = D-Mart founder stake.
- Jhunjhunwala: borrowed client money early; 1992 bear trade; Titan ~1/3 of wealth.
- Prop firms/FPIs: speed, algos, option-selling at scale; took ~₹58k cr from F&O in FY26
  while individuals lost (SEBI).
Knowledge alone is commoditised. Edge = information processed differently, over time,
plus temperament and survival.

## Our combined edge thesis — "Lynch at scale" (proposed 2026-11-19, pending user input)
**Claude's edges**
1. Exhaustive reading at scale: every annual report, concall, credit-rating rationale,
   DRHP, SEBI order for ~1,500 small caps, every quarter.
2. Change detection over time: promise-vs-delivery tracking of each management team
   (guidance credibility score). Funds don't do this for ₹500 cr companies.
3. Cross-domain second-order chains (e.g. US hyperscaler capex → NA equipment demand →
   Indian parts supplier like Uniparts) — found on purpose, not by accident.
4. Discipline enforcement: kill criteria, pre-mortems, decision journal.

**Claude's limits:** no physical world, no memory unless written here, number errors
unless verified, no proprietary data.
